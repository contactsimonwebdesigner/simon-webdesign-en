04 / FAQ

Before You
Get in Touch.

Clear and simple. And if something is missing, just send me a message.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the scope, content and features. After a short discussion, I'll prepare a tailored quote so you know what to expect.

How long does building a website take?

The timeline depends on the scope and how ready the content is. We'll agree on a delivery date before work begins.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Absolutely. I design layouts, text sizes and navigation for smaller screens as well, so the website works on everyday devices.

Can I request changes later?

Of course. Content, sections and features can be updated as your business grows. We'll discuss the scope of any future changes.

What if I don't have text or photos yet?

That's fine. We'll decide what materials we need and can start with the structure and visual direction.
